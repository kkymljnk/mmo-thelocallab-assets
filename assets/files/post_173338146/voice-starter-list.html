The rules (read before you use a voice)

  • These voices are CC0 donations to Kyutai's Unmute Voice Donation Project. Anyone may use them for any purpose, commercial or not. Read the donation terms (PDF).
  • Donors can withdraw. Before you publish anything made with a voice, open its link again and check that the file is still in Kyutai's repo. If the page says the file is not found, do not publish with that voice.
  • Never name the speaker, and never suggest that they endorse your content.
  • Label realistic AI voices as AI: tick YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting, and use Meta's "AI info" label.
  • Follow Kyutai's Acceptable Use Policy. The donation terms make every use of these voices subject to it.

Use a voice in the members workflow

  1. Pick a voice from the lists below.
  2. Open its link. Kyutai's page plays the sample and has a download link. Download the .wav file.
  3. In the workflow, switch on "Clone a voice from a sample" (the switches in group 5) and load the .wav into "Voice sample".
  4. Paste its transcript (shown next to the voice below) into "Transcript", word for word: the model learns the voice from the sample and its text together.
  5. Name the voice in "Save it as a character voice" (e.g. your character's name) and run once. Press R, switch "Use a saved voice" on and the clone group off, and pick the name: every shot now uses that voice.
  6. Before you publish, re-check the link (see the rules above).

How to read the list

  • Each label shows the list number, how the voice sounds (male-sounding, female-sounding or ambiguous), the age band it sounds like, and its timbre. Labels and notes come from our automatic listening filter and describe the sample, not the donor.
  • Two age bands (for example 26-40 or 41-60) mean our two listening passes heard different ages.
  • "Notes from our listening filter" lists processing sounds the filter heard (for example metallic or muffled). Every one was rated slight or none. "none flagged" means it heard none.
  • Transcripts are automatic and can contain mistakes: listen to the sample and fix the transcript before you paste it. Voices whose samples include a name or personal details are not on this list.
  • Each link opens Kyutai's cleaned version of the sample (the file name ends in _enhanced.wav). The original recording is in the same folder. Every sample is 10 seconds long.
  • Within each list, voices are sorted by voice type, then age band.
